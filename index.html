Welcome to our shop

For years, our little shop in Alagapuram, Periya Pudhur has been the place neighbours meet for a hot cup of tea, fresh snacks and daily needs. From morning tea to evening groceries and household items, everything is here at honest prices.

About Us

Our Story

Murugan Tea Stall & Malligai Store began as a small tea stall and grew with the trust of our neighbours. Today we serve fresh tea and snacks, stock groceries and vegetables, and keep household essentials ready so you never need to travel far.

We believe in fair prices, fresh products and a friendly smile for every customer, from children buying biscuits to elders who stop by for their daily tea.
